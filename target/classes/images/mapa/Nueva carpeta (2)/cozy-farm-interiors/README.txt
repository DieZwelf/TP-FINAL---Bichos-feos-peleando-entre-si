Cozy Farm Interiors — free 16 x 16 top-down farmhouse interior pack

Floors, walls and 28 pieces of furniture for the inside of a cozy
farmhouse, in the same palette and style as Cozy Farm. 203 PNG files (1x, 2x, 4x).

tiles/      6 seamless floors, 16 x 16: honey, walnut and grey wood planks,
            kitchen tiles, stone and carpet
walls/      15 front walls, 16 x 32 (beam, wallpaper, wainscot): cream, sage,
            rose and blue wallpaper and a log cabin wall, each plain, with a
            window and with a door
frame/      room frame (the top of the walls seen from above): nw n ne w e sw
            s se plus 4 inner corners. Transparent inside and outside: draw it
            over the floor and walls.
furniture/  bed, table, chair, stool, wood-stove, kitchen-counter, sink, cupboard, bookshelf, wardrobe, dresser, armchair, sofa, rocking-chair, chest, flour-sack, cooking-pot, potted-plant, flower-vase, floor-lamp, rug-round, rug-runner, wall-clock, painting, wall-shelf, hanging-herbs, cat-bed
            and a stone fireplace with 3 fire frames (fireplace-frame1..3 and
            a 96 x 32 strip; play at ~8 fps). Furniture sits on the bottom
            edge of a canvas sized in whole tiles.
tileset/    floors + frame, and walls, on one sheet each
sheets/     all the furniture on one sheet
examples/   a farmhouse kitchen and living room built from this pack (the
            farmer and grandma are from Cozy Farm)

Want the outside? Cozy Farm has the farmhouse, barn, crops, animals and 8
villagers: https://aquumgifts.itch.io/cozy-farm (free Lite version too).

Made in code and text art with a fixed palette.
